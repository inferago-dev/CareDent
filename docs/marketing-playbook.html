PHASE 01

This week

For a local supplier, the Google Business Profile is worth more than the entire website. It is what appears in the map box when someone searches “dental chair dealer near me”.

01

Claim the Google Business Profile

Go to business.google.com and search for Care Dent. Claim it if it exists; create it if it does not.

  • Primary category: Dental equipment supplier
  • Additional categories: Medical equipment supplier, Equipment repair service
  • Name, address, phone, hours: exactly as in the box above
  • Services: add each one — installation, maintenance, breakdown repair, site assessment, safety inspection
  • Website link: caredent.in

Verification usually arrives by postcard to the Mugalivakkam address, and takes one to two weeks. Start it now — everything else waits on it.

Done whenSearching “Care Dent Chennai” on your phone shows the business panel on the right.
02

Put your own photographs on the profile

Profiles with real photographs get contacted far more often than profiles without. You already have the material.

  • The clinic installations already on your Gallery page
  • An engineer working on a chair — the work itself, not a posed shot
  • The equipment you stock, on your own premises
  • One clear photograph of Mr. Sivakumar for the profile

Avoid stock images entirely. A slightly imperfect real photo of your own work outperforms a polished stock one, because dentists can tell the difference.

Done whenAt least ten genuine photographs are live on the profile.
03

Ask your first five customers for a review

This is the single highest-value hour in this document. Reviews decide which of three suppliers a dentist calls.

Ask on the day the work goes well — right after a successful installation or a repair that saved their morning. Not a week later. Send the link on WhatsApp while you are still in the clinic.

WhatsApp message you can copyDr. [Name], glad the [chair / compressor] is running well again. If you have a minute, a short review would really help other dentists find us: [paste your Google review link] Thank you — Sivakumar, Care Dent

Get your review link from the Business Profile dashboard under “Ask for reviews”. Send it to people who genuinely had a good experience, and send it in whichever language you normally speak with them.

Done whenFive genuine reviews are live. Then keep going — aim for two to four every month, forever.
PHASE 02

The next two weeks

Now connect the website to Google properly, and get listed where Indian dentists actually search for suppliers.

04

Set up Google Search Console

Go to search.google.com/search-console, add caredent.in, verify ownership, then submit the sitemap.

Sitemap URL to submithttps://caredent.in/sitemap.xml

This tells Google every page exists instead of waiting for it to find them. It is also where you will later see which searches actually bring people to you — real data, not guesswork.

Done whenThe sitemap shows “Success” and pages start appearing under Indexing.
05

List on the directories dentists use

Indian dental buyers search these before Google, especially for equipment.

  • IndiaMART — the most important one for equipment enquiries. Free listing; list each product category
  • JustDial — heavily used for local service searches in Chennai
  • TradeIndia and Sulekha — worth the twenty minutes each
  • Google Maps — already handled by Phase 1

Use the same name, address and phone every single time. Inconsistent listings actively damage your ranking, so it is better to skip a directory than to list it with a shortened address.

Done whenFour listings are live, each carrying identical details and linking to caredent.in.
06

Switch to WhatsApp Business

Most of your enquiries will arrive here. The free Business app does things the normal app cannot.

  • Catalogue: add your chairs and equipment with photos — people browse it and ask about specific models
  • Away message: for after 7 PM and Sundays, so nobody thinks they were ignored
  • Quick replies: save your standard answers for price enquiries and service bookings
  • Business profile: address, hours and caredent.in, so the link is always one tap away
Away messageThank you for contacting Care Dent. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM, and will reply first thing. For an urgent equipment breakdown, please call +91 94441 53599.
Done whenThe catalogue is populated and the away message is switched on.
PHASE 03

Every month, permanently

Small and consistent beats large and occasional. Thirty minutes a month keeps all of the above working.

07

Keep asking for reviews

Two to four new reviews a month, every month. A profile that gained twelve reviews last year and none this year looks like a business in decline.

Make it a habit tied to an event, not a task on a list: every completed installation and every repair that went well ends with the WhatsApp message from step 3. Reply to every review you receive, including any critical one — calmly, with what you did about it. Dentists read the replies more carefully than the reviews.

Done whenIt has become automatic and you no longer need this document to remember it.
08

Post to the Business Profile

One post a month. Google favours profiles that show recent activity, and it takes five minutes.

  • A clinic you just finished installing, with a photo
  • A piece of equipment now in stock
  • A link to one of the guides on the website
  • A seasonal reminder — pre-monsoon compressor and suction checks, for instance
Done whenThere is a post from within the last thirty days, every time you look.
09

Add one guide a quarter

The website has a Guides section with three articles. Four a year is plenty.

Write about what dentists actually phone you to ask. If three people asked the same question last month, that question is your next guide. You have thirty years of answers that nobody else in Chennai can write — that is the whole advantage, and no competitor can copy it.

Done whenA fourth guide is live. Send the text to your developer to add.

Things that will actively damage you

  • Buying reviews. Google detects it and removes the profile. It is the fastest way to lose everything above.
  • Fake testimonials on the website. Named doctors who do not exist were already removed once — do not let them come back.
  • A different address on different sites. Inconsistency is read as two businesses, and neither ranks.
  • Anyone promising “page 1 in 30 days”. They will use tactics that get the site penalised, and leave you to it.
  • Star ratings nobody gave you. The 4.9-star ratings on the product pages are not from real customers. They stay off Google's structured data for exactly this reason — and they are still visible to any dentist reading the page.
